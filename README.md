# Global-Multilateral-AI-Governance-Framework-
Technical and strategic repository for the establishment of a Multilateral Global Governance Committee on AI, coordinated by ODET (UN).

Our mission is to translate abstract ethical principles into mandatory technical standards to mitigate AI-enabled violence, focusing on the safety of women and children. This repository hosts technical white papers, risk assessment methodologies (Red/Blue Teaming), and digital literacy frameworks developed by our international working group.

Key Focus Areas: Technical Resilience & Auditing. Content Provenance, Framework, Metadata. Global Digital Literacy Curriculums.  Multilateral Policy Alignment (UN/ITU/UNESCO/UN WOMEN).

Our Team & Foundational Vision

The conceptual foundation for this framework originated from a dedicated effort to address critical safety gaps in Artificial Intelligence development, specifically concerning the protection of women and children. This journey began with active advocacy and direct engagement with AI startups and technology corporations, calling for systemic changes to prioritize human well-being.

Recognizing that effective governance requires more than advocacy, the initiative evolved into independent field research and strategic policy development. This process led to the refinement of our multilateral governance model and the assembly of a multidisciplinary team of experts committed to transforming these insights into global technical standards.
